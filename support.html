FAQ

I don't feel anything. Check that System Haptics is on (Settings > Sounds & Haptics) and pick "Strong" in the game's settings.

How do I find every ending? Play again. And again. Every choice matters, especially the ones that seem harmless.

Can I reset my progress? Yes: Settings > Reset all endings.
